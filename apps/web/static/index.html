Language and game

Game mode
The port's extras: the fast forward, an autosave, 31 achievements and play records, and settings for battle animations, damage numbers, auto text and weapon reach, also in the game's pause menu. Saves stay in the cartridge's own format.

Classic plays as the cartridge did. Enhanced adds the port's extras, such as the fast forward and the achievements, set below.

Enhanced mode

  • The attack scenes in battles; without them a battle goes faster.

  • The damage each unit takes, as a number under it for a moment.

  • Text boxes go on by themselves once read. SELECT turns it on or off while one shows.

  • Saves on each change of map, into a slot of its own that Continue lists first.

  • Shops and the weapons menu draw what a weapon reaches in battle. SELECT turns from the front row to the back.

Fast forward

How fast the game plays while Space, the right stick or the pad's >> is held.

The game's own pause menu changes these too, and the page remembers them.
