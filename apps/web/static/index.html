Re:Zoids Saga

An unofficial port of Zoids Saga (Game Boy Advance) to play in the browser.

Choose your Zoids Saga ROM (Japan, Rev 1), or drop it here.

It is read in this browser and kept only here, so you choose it once; it is never sent anywhere.

Saves

Kept in this browser. Export one for an emulator (.sav), RetroArch (.srm) or as the cartridge would hold it, or import one from them.

Cloud saves

Sign in with your email to keep your saves and achievements in the cloud and play them in any browser. What is kept, and how to delete it.

Signed in as . The saves sync when the page opens, every minute while you play, and when you leave it.

Arrows move, X = A, Z = B, Return = START, Backspace = SELECT, A = L, S = R, M mutes, Space holds the enhanced mode's fast forward; gamepads work too.

Works offline once loaded, and can be installed from the browser's menu. Free software under the GPL-3.0: source code. Not affiliated with the game's owners. Version __VERSION__.
