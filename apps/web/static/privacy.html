Privacy

What this page keeps, where, and how to delete it.

In your browser

The ROM you choose is read and kept in this browser alone (its database), so you choose it once. It is never sent anywhere: not to this site, not to the cloud. The saves, the achievements, the translation you chose and the page's options are kept in this browser too. Forget the ROM removes the ROM; clearing the site's data in the browser removes everything.

In the cloud, only if you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, the cloud keeps:

  • your email address, to send you the sign-in link and tell your account apart;
  • your saves and achievements, each with what it holds (level, area, money, time played) and when it was stored, and the last ten versions of each save, so one can be taken back;
  • the ROM's fingerprint (its SHA-1), to keep the saves of each ROM apart. It is a number computed from the ROM, not the ROM itself.

Nothing else is collected: no tracking, no advertising, no analytics. The data is used for the cloud saves alone and is never sold or shared. Only you can reach your saves.

Deleting it

Delete my account, in the page's cloud section, deletes your account, your email address and every save and version kept in the cloud, at once. The saves in your browser stay. Sign out only stops the syncing.
